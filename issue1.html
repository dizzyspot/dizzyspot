ISSUE 1

INAUGURATION OF THE CRYSTALLINE ORDER

Elora Sander

reporting from Lukia

Dear citizens of Crystalleum, we are pleased to share the first issue of our news report, the Adamante, with you. Today marks the official end of the Fifth Crystal War and the inauguration of the Crystalline Order. The Crystalline Order is led by the most powerful man in Crystalleum, Archmage Elyseus, who has created a magical arena to settle conflicts between nations instead of the real world, hence preventing unnecessary deaths. It shall also prevent the permanent deaths of the world’s most prized and best fighters whilst providing entertaining battles for the people of different nations to watch. The matches shall be led by Judges from different regions who will be paired with a Chosen, to fight on the Fields of Eternity.

Elyseus Weiss - “A man who has witnessed far too much suffering. Having reached the pinnacle of magic, he has created an arena that defies the natural balance of life and death.”

DISCOVERY OF MEGA-CRYSTAL IN MAJMAR

Gatsy Barrell

reporting from Gernim

A new Mega-Crystal has been discovered in the Frozen Wastelands of Majmar by Gernim explorers . We met with some nomads who seemed to be unaware of its existence. They were not very welcoming, to say the least. According to Skye, a young explorer from Gernim, the crystal was encased in a thick layer of ice. Gernim industrials plan to mine the mineral, however mage Isendel from one of Majmar’s nomad troupes contests this decision as “unethical” and “impudent” towards the people of her clan. The Fifth War has just ended and tensions are already rising between Majmar and Gernim.

“Without the Gernim explorers’ efforts, the Mega-Crystal would never have been discovered,” said Skye. “The least they could do is share part of the findings.”

Negotiations are underway but Isendel, the self-proclaimed Majmar leader, refuses to give away mining rights to Gernim over the Crystal.

Erin Dusson, spokesman of the Council of Gernim, mentions that Isendel is dramatising the issue to gain political power in Majmar. “There are no official leaders in Majmar. Isendel is just trying to gain popularity among her people.”

Because of the political clash between Gernim and Majmar, the Crystalline Order has decided to step in. They are investigating the nature of the Crystal with the explorers' help, to the dismay of Isendel.

THE CAGE

By Duo Meister

All the latest news about the Fields of Eternity’s Chosens!

Etheren, the Calm Before the Storm

Etheren is the Crystalline Order’s first Chosen. He has the face of an angel, but do not be fooled! There’s more to his good looks than meets the eye! Clad in Lukian armor, he is a one-man army. Quite literally. We were able to ask him a few questions.

Duo: As the Crystalline Order's first Chosen, how are you preparing for any future matches?

Etheren: There is an Archmage by the name of “Splicer” who made a perfect copy of me.

Duo: Oh so, you’re saying that you’ve been fighting yourself? Hmm! Quite interesting!

Duo: What goes into your thoughts as you prepare for battle? I mean, what’s your approach on this?

Etheren: I must defeat all enemies, regardless of who they are.

Duo: I want to know more about Etheren, not the Calm Before the Storm, but who he is deep down. Tell us a bit about yourself!

Etheren: I have nothing to say about myself.

Duo: Aww… you’re being too humble.

Duo: Do you think you could solo a whole team by summoning your warrior spirits

Etheren: This is not a question I can answer.

Duo: Oh, I bet you're being mysterious! I guess the only way to find out is in a live match!

So this is it for the latest update on The Cage. Check back for future releases as more and more Chosens join the crew!
