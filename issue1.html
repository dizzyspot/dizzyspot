The Fields of Eternity: An innovative arena that will change the world

Elora Sander

reporting from Lukia

Dear citizens of Crystalleum, we are pleased to share the first issue of our news report, the Adamante, with you. Today marks the official end of the Fifth Crystal War and the inauguration of the Crystalline Order. The Crystalline Order is led by the most powerful man in Crystalleum, Archmage Elyseus, who has created a virtual arena to settle conflicts between nations instead of the real world, hence preventing unnecessary deaths. It shall also prevent the permanent deaths of the world’s most prized and best fighters whilst providing entertaining battles for the people of different nations to watch. The matches shall be led by Judges from different regions who will be paired with a Chosen, to fight on the Fields of Eternity.

Elyseus Weiss - “A man who has witnessed far too much suffering. Having reached the pinnacle of magic, he has created an arena that defies the natural balance of life and death.”
